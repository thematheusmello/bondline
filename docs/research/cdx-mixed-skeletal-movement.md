# Moving skeletal atoms and their printed labels together in CDX

Reviewed 2026-11-20. The selected Node owns its 2DPosition, and its directly contained label Text owns the bounding rectangle. Binary positions store vertical and horizontal signed 16.16 words. The rectangle stores top, left, bottom and right. A chemical Node's position locates its element token. The existing checked box constrains the actual printed label. A common signed-grid translation preserves this relationship without recomputing glyph geometry. [2D position](https://iupac.github.io/IUPAC-FAIRSpec/cdx_sdk/properties/2DPosition.htm) · [Text bounding rectangle](https://iupac.github.io/IUPAC-FAIRSpec/cdx_sdk/properties/BoundingBox.htm) · [Binary and XML coordinates](https://iupac.github.io/IUPAC-FAIRSpec/cdx_sdk/DataType/CDXCoordinates.htm)

Bondline rounds the requested displacement once with ties to even. Each selected hidden atom changes its position. A selected printed atom changes its position and all four edges of its existing box. Owners are identified by their actual opening records, including when different Fragments reuse raw IDs or a Node position follows its Text. This applies only to the complete hidden-carbon and explicitly qualified printed-heteroatom profiles. Selection acts on the current source snapshot, and a zero-grid move retains the snapshot and redo. The candidate must re-import to the expected entire model in the same profile and fit the whole page after bond clearance. Other source and chemistry edit grants are independent.
