# Skeletal carbon on a binary CDX page

Reviewed 2026-11-20. The SDK assigns 0x0442 to terminal carbon labels at degree zero or one, and 0x0443 to nonterminal carbon labels at degree greater than one. Both use CDXBooleanImplied. Presence has a zero-byte payload and means true. Absence means false. A stored byte 00 is invalid for this type. The CDXML spelling and its root defaults do not establish a binary root-to-Node inheritance rule. Bondline admits zero-length flags only on their own Nodes in this closed display profile. A true value for the inactive degree class remains in the model. [Property table](https://iupac.github.io/IUPAC-FAIRSpec/cdx_sdk/TableOfProperties.htm), [binary boolean encodings](https://iupac.github.io/IUPAC-FAIRSpec/cdx_sdk/DataType/CDXBoolean.htm).

A contained Text supplies the Node's drawing. With no contained Text, chemical fields can supply a depiction. A missing Element means carbon. An omitted NumHydrogens calls for the minimum H needed for valence. Bondline restricts this calculation to neutral, natural, nonradical carbon with plain single bonds. A stored H count must agree with a bond-order sum plus H of four. Omitted H and the absent flags remain absent in the authored model. The source-qualified view separately resolves counts and implied visibility. [Node and child Text](https://iupac.github.io/IUPAC-FAIRSpec/cdx_sdk/Node.htm), [Element](https://iupac.github.io/IUPAC-FAIRSpec/cdx_sdk/properties/Node_Element.htm), [attached H](https://iupac.github.io/IUPAC-FAIRSpec/cdx_sdk/properties/Atom_NumHydrogens.htm).

A candidate must account for every record on one direct Poster page. It needs explicit dimensions, black foreground and white paper palette entries, line width and margin. Every node is unlabelled and must qualify for hidden ink at its current position. The existing renderer handles isolation, two-carbon components, incident angles and paper limits. It retains chemical hits at hidden vertices. An active true flag requires visible text and falls outside this glyphless profile. The [IUPAC 2008 graphical recommendations](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/drawing/drawing.html#GR211) guide the depiction policy. The exact width, visibility, bounds checks and read-only admission are Bondline decisions. [Existing skeletal depiction rules](skeletal-carbon-display.md), [terminal-carbon policy](terminal-carbon-labels.md).

The separate display profile grants exact-source Open, view and unchanged CDX Save. A detached source, further chemistry or caption editing, foreign format conversion, unknown containment and root overrides keep their existing guards. Opaque or unsupported sources retain the old elemental fallback or the original decode error. Original fixtures pin the binary source, the projected data, noncanonical choices of object ID and independent chemical and optical evidence.
