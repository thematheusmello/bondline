## Public basis and original policy

The primary SDK [coordinate types](https://iupac.github.io/IUPAC-FAIRSpec/cdx_sdk/DataType/CDXCoordinates.htm)
give CDXML points in x/y order and rectangles in left/top/right/bottom order, in points
with positive axes right/down. Binary CDX ordering/scale differs and is not extended here.
[2DPosition](https://iupac.github.io/IUPAC-FAIRSpec/cdx_sdk/properties/2DPosition.htm)
distinguishes elemental-node placement from caption baselines; this work changes neither
convention. [BoundingBox](https://iupac.github.io/IUPAC-FAIRSpec/cdx_sdk/properties/BoundingBox.htm)
describes the graphical enclosure. Translating a known node/ink rectangle together is
an affine operation, not evidence for interpreting unsupported child Text p/defaults.

Public GUIDE25 printed88,115–116,120 supplies atom/bond/component selection, Shift
addition/removal, marquee/Select All, drag and1pt/Shift10pt nudges. Its existing public
URL/PDF/text pins are in [native selection acceptance](native-selection-acceptance.md).
Those version25 facts do not prove every26.1/platform flow. Press-latched modifiers,
anchor-based marquee, selection/history ownership and final physical/source refusal
remain original checked policies. No reference binary, export or asset is used.
