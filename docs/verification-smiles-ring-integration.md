## Delivered contract and independent basis

`neutral_simple_ring_v1` / **Neutral ring structures** accepts one actual connected
unicyclic graph, whose sole cycle has3..8 atoms and Single edges. Attached trees retain
the existing neutral, nonisotopic H/C/N/O/F/Cl/Br/I fixed-H policy, physical-H nodes and
supported Single/Double/Triple configurations. Total atoms are at most64, checked before
normalization/search. This is a resource ceiling, not a fit promise. Cycle multiple bonds,
larger/polycyclic graphs, components, aromaticity, charges, isotopes and unsupported
double-bond configurations refuse as a whole.

The [preimplementation contract](research/smiles-ring-layout-plan.md),
[pinned public-source review](research/smiles-ring-layout-sources.md) and
[independent graph/geometry/oracle record](verification-smiles-ring-layout.md) distinguish
published notation/depiction conventions from original Bondline policies. Actual graph
connectivity, E=V and the peeled degree-two core determine the cycle; ring/dot tokens
are not a topology shortcut. Regular polygons, contextual exterior branches and bounded
phase/search choices retain every actual bond, directed identity and authored H count.
Minimum atom-center x/y becomes the requested origin by rigid translation. Bond length
and paper never shrink. Actual-edge/intersection, full literal glyph/stroke/paper and
same-authored-count literal/resolved Scene certificates remain mandatory.

The one30-command registry retains all eight required scalar parameters. The optional
profile has three closed values; omitted/default serialization and legacy receipts remain
exact. Source notation ownership, create-only publication, per-Engine Save reservation,
registered worker retirement, cancellation/stale/close checks and truthful historical
receipts remain shared by CLI, stdio MCP, authenticated loopback and native UI. Conversion
never changes current/saved source, history, association or native Save/close authority.
Explicit Open of the generated CDXML is a separate action. Native profile activations
replay at their input-event positions and cannot relabel pending/running work.
